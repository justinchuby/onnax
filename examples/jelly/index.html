Execution details & limitations

Waiting for a WebGPU adapter.

CPU execution-provider fallback is disabled. Generated H·v graphs and eight CG slots stay on the GPU. Two speculative Newton proposals share one packed readback; the CPU checks every accepted step with an Armijo line search. Rejected speculation is discarded. Small position readbacks feed Canvas 2D rendering. Timings are end-to-end wall time, not GPU-only kernel time. Simulation time does not catch up to wall time on slower devices.

The automated browser smoke test also counts actual WebGPU compute dispatches. Adapter identity and live timings above describe this browser, not a promised framerate.
